[WIDGET 3] BUSINESS SITUATION
[WIDGET 4] Huge volume of Data to synchronize, limited bandwidth.
[WIDGET 5] The core activities of the Karnataka State Beverages Corporation Ltd.(KSBCL) are to procure liquor and spirit from manufacturers/suppliers and sell the same to wholesalers/licensees. Thus data consolidation from the 53+ branches at the HO was an important and required task. Also daily corporate MIS / DSS had to be generated. Thus a system to manage their business processes covering these tasks apart from Finance, Sales &amp; Distribution, Purchasing &amp; Inventory modules was the need of the hour.
[WIDGET 6] CHALLENGES
[WIDGET 7] Key challenge was to provide a system which is easy to use due to the nonavailability of skilled operators &amp; lack of tech skill at the branches. No broadband connectivity at many of the branches, even the dial-up connections present were weak. Huge volume of data from all the branches had to be synchronized at the HO, with this infrastructure, on a daily basis. (1000+ transactions from each branch daily) To deploy the complete solution in less than 3 months elapsed time.
[WIDGET 8] TECHNOLOGY AND TOOLS
[WIDGET 9] PROFIT 5RM 6.2 P5 Sync Server/Client Microsoft .Net Framework, ASP .Net, VB .Net, C# Oracle 10g Delphi Microsoft Windows Server 2008 Linux
[WIDGET 10] SOLUTION
[WIDGET 11] Implemented the following modules:
[WIDGET 12] Financial Accounting: Apart from Depot updating the central server this module at HO is also been used for making regular financial accounting entries like – Payment / Journals / Contra / Debit – Credit Note etc …
[WIDGET 13] Vendor Payment Calculates – Brand wise , Supplier Wise , Item Wise Sales Quantity Picks the Purchase Rate based on Sales Date and Calculates the Purchase value supplier wise Adjustment towards excise duties and other recoveries Generates suppliers / Bank Wise Advice for transfer of Funds
[WIDGET 14] Demurrage Calculation Calculates – Age of Stock more than 90 days Applies Demurrage rate per case item wise , supplier wise where ever applicable
[WIDGET 15] Pricing Basic Declared Price – Excise + Additional Excise Duty is calculated Margin is added and KSBCL Purchase and selling Price is derived MRP of Retailer Per Bottle is Calculated o Purchase Based on weekly payment module a detailed report of Purchase value is generated Interface for entering Purchase Voucher
[WIDGET 16] OFS / TON 3rd party generates the Order for Supply [ OFS ] and provides it to our database which is converted into actual data and sent through synchronization to respective depot TON Interface is provided between depots On approval the document is populated to both Send / Receive depots.
[WIDGET 17] Sales Generation of Sales Indent after confirming the availability of Items Checklist for Loading [Materials] Generation of Invoice – After validating the availability of Funds in Party Accounts and calculation of TCS [Tax Collected at Source]
[WIDGET 18] BENEFITS
[WIDGET 19] Online information available to stakeholder Excise, retailers, manufacturers, and government Information highway opened to manufacturers
[WIDGET 20] Basic &amp; Consolidated view at HO of all depots Sales Inventory Purchase Receipts Payments Financial Accounting
[WIDGET 21] Accurate &amp; Online inventory information Decision-support MIS on Supplies, stock movement and fund management Issue OFS (order for supply) with digital signature over the web.
